FokusAkademik + Profesional + Teknologi/IT
Durasi12 bulan | 6 hari/minggu | 2–3 jam/hari (≈ 720–1.080 jam total)
MetodologiComprehensible Input (Krashen), Spaced Repetition (SRS), Deliberate Practice (Ericsson), Shadowing Technique, Output-Input Cycle, Interleaving
Prinsip evidence-based yang dipakai
  • Spaced Repetition — vocabulary diulang dengan interval meningkat (Anki/SRS) agar masuk long-term memory.
  • Comprehensible Input +1 — materi listening/reading sedikit di atas level saat ini, bukan terlalu mudah/sulit.
  • Output Practice — speaking & writing wajib tiap hari (bukan hanya pasif input).
  • Shadowing — teknik terbukti efektif untuk pronunciation & fluency (dipakai interpreter profesional).
  • Interleaving — skill dicampur tiap hari (bukan blok 1 skill saja) agar transfer lebih baik.
  • Testing Effect — self-quiz & mock test rutin lebih efektif daripada re-reading pasif.

Peta Grammar

Urutan ini disusun berdasarkan dependency logis (topik dasar harus dikuasai dulu sebelum topik lanjutan yang membutuhkannya) dan kebutuhan TOEFL Structure & Written Expression.

UrutanTopik GrammarBulanAlasan Urutan
1Review Tenses (12 tenses + perfect continuous)1Fondasi semua kalimat; sering salah di level intermediate
2Parts of Speech & Sentence Pattern (S-V-O-C)1Wajib sebelum masuk clause kompleks
3Passive Voice2Umum di academic writing & dokumentasi teknis
4Reported Speech (Direct→Indirect)2Dibutuhkan untuk retelling & meeting recap
5Relative Clause (Adjective Clause)3Dasar untuk clause kompleks berikutnya
6Adjective Clause Reduction3Lanjutan langsung dari relative clause
7Noun Clause4Sering muncul di TOEFL Structure
8Adverb Clause (time, reason, contrast, purpose)4Melengkapi 3 jenis clause (noun/adj/adverb)
9Conditional Sentences (0–3 + mixed)5Butuh pemahaman tense yang kuat (langkah 1)
10Modal Verbs (deduction, obligation, advice, past modals)5Sering di speaking opinion & writing formal
11Gerund vs Infinitive6Detail yang sering salah di level lanjut
12Reduced Clause (participle phrase)6Butuh clause dasar (langkah 5–8) sudah matang
13Inversion (negative adverbial, conditional inversion)7Advanced structure, sering di TOEFL tricky items
14Parallel Structure & Comparative/Superlative lanjutan7Sering jadi jebakan di Written Expression
15Advanced Sentence Structure (compound-complex, cleft sentence)8Menggabungkan semua clause sebelumnya
16Academic Grammar (nominalization, hedging, formal register)8–9Level tertinggi, untuk jurnal & laporan formal
17Full Integration Review (semua topik dicampur random)9–12Interleaved practice untuk retensi jangka panjang

Cara belajar tiap topik (siklus 1 minggu per topik utama):

  1. Hari 1–2: Pahami konsep + contoh (grammar book/video) → catat pola.
  2. Hari 3: Latihan soal (50 soal minimal) + cek jawaban, analisis kesalahan.
  3. Hari 4: Gunakan pola tersebut dalam 10 kalimat sendiri (writing).
  4. Hari 5: Gunakan pola tersebut secara lisan (speaking practice/AI roleplay).
  5. Hari 6: Kuis campuran (topik minggu ini + topik minggu lalu) → interleaving.

AI Companion

AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) bisa berperan sebagai 8 jenis "guru" berbeda. Gunakan prompt di bawah, ganti bagian [...] sesuai kebutuhan.

1. Speaking Partner

"Act as a friendly native English speaker. Have a natural 15-minute conversation with me about [topik]. Ask me follow-up questions, don't correct me during the conversation — just respond naturally like a real chat. At the end, give me 3 corrections on grammar/vocabulary I used incorrectly."

2. Grammar Checker

"Check the grammar of this text: '[teks saya]'. List every error, explain WHY it's wrong (the grammar rule), and show the corrected version. Then give me 3 similar practice sentences using the same rule."

3. Pronunciation Evaluator

"I will describe how I pronounced this word/sentence: '[kata/kalimat]'. My pronunciation attempt was: [jelaskan usaha pengucapan, misal per suku kata]. Tell me the correct IPA transcription, common mistakes Indonesian speakers make with this word, and 3 minimal pairs to practice."

*(Catatan: untuk evaluasi suara real-time, kombinasikan dengan Elsa Speak/YouGlish; AI teks tidak bisa dengar suara asli, tapi bisa bantu analisis fonetik & drill.)*

4. TOEFL Tutor

"Act as a TOEFL ITP tutor. Give me 10 Structure & Written Expression questions at [easy/medium/hard] level, focused on [topik grammar, misal: reduced clause]. After I answer, grade me and explain each wrong answer in detail."

5. Vocabulary Trainer

"Give me 15 new intermediate-advanced vocabulary words related to [tema, misal: software development]. For each word: definition in English, 1 example sentence, 1 collocation, and 1 synonym. Then quiz me on 5 of them at the end."

6. Interview Simulator

"Act as a hiring manager interviewing me for a [posisi, misal: Backend Developer] role. Ask me 5 common interview questions one at a time, wait for my answer before moving to the next. At the end, give feedback on my English (grammar, vocabulary, confidence of expression) and content (using STAR method)."

7. Debate Partner

"Let's debate. I will argue [posisi saya] on the topic '[topik]'. You argue the opposite side. Respond with strong counterarguments so I have to think and respond in English. After 5 exchanges, give me feedback on my argumentation and language use."

8. Essay Evaluator

"Evaluate this essay: '[essay saya]'. Score it based on: grammar accuracy, vocabulary range, coherence/organization, and task achievement. Give a score out of 100 and specific suggestions for improvement, with rewritten examples of my weakest sentences."

Bonus — Thinking in English Coach

"For the next conversation, only respond in English, and occasionally ask me to describe my current surroundings, feelings, or plans in English, like a casual friend checking in — this is to train my 'thinking in English' habit."

Tips Konsistensi

  1. Habit Stacking — tempelkan belajar bahasa Inggris ke rutinitas yang sudah ada (misal: SRS review sambil sarapan, listening saat perjalanan/olahraga).
  2. Jangan Skip, Kurangi — kalau capek, jangan skip total; kurangi jadi 30 menit ("minimum viable session") daripada 0 menit. Konsistensi > intensitas.
  3. Rekam Progress — simpan rekaman speaking tiap bulan; dengarkan ulang rekaman bulan pertama saat bulan ke-6 & ke-12 — ini bukti nyata progress yang sangat memotivasi.
  4. Gamifikasi — pakai streak counter (misal di Anki atau notes app), reward diri sendiri tiap milestone quarterly tercapai.
  5. Accountability Partner — cari partner belajar (teman/komunitas online) untuk saling cek progress mingguan, atau laporkan progress ke AI companion tiap minggu.
  6. Fokus pada Proses, Bukan Sempurna — target bukan "tidak boleh salah", tapi "belajar dari 1 kesalahan besar tiap hari" (Error Log).
  7. Variasikan Konten — kalau bosan dengan 1 sumber (misal VOA), ganti podcast/channel lain di level yang sama; jangan berhenti belajar hanya karena bosan sumbernya.
  8. Evaluasi Ulang Rencana Tiap Kuartal — setelah simulasi TOEFL kuartalan, sesuaikan rencana bulan berikutnya jika ada skill yang tertinggal (gunakan waktu ekstra untuk remedial, kurangi topik baru sementara).
  9. Sleep & Spacing Matter — SRS efektif karena otak butuh waktu untuk konsolidasi memori saat tidur; jangan begadang belajar maraton lalu skip beberapa hari.
  10. Ingat "Kenapa" — tempelkan tujuan akhir (kerja IT, interview, TOEFL 560+) di tempat terlihat; review tujuan ini tiap awal bulan sebelum mulai matriks bulan baru.
Selamat memulai perjalanan 12 bulan ini 🚀

Dokumen ini adalah kurikulum hidup — sesuaikan intensitas kalau ada minggu sibuk, tapi pertahankan struktur interleaving skill harian + SRS vocabulary + TOEFL practice terjadwal. Itulah yang membuatnya evidence-based, bukan sekadar jadwal biasa.
